# Two Pointers Pattern

## Overview

The Two Pointers technique uses two indices to traverse arrays or strings efficiently. It is commonly used to reduce time complexity from O(n²) to O(n).

## Sub Patterns

### 1. Opposite Direction Pointers
**Time Complexity:** O(n)
**Space Complexity:** O(1)

Examples:
- LC 167 Two Sum II

---

### 2. Fast and Slow Pointers
**Time Complexity:** O(n)
**Space Complexity:** O(1)

Examples:
- LC 26 Remove Duplicates from Sorted Array

---

### 3. Sliding Window (Variable Size)
**Time Complexity:** O(n)
**Space Complexity:** O(k)

Examples:
- LC 3 Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters

---

### 4. Three Pointers
**Time Complexity:** O(n²)
**Space Complexity:** O(1)

Examples:
- LC 15 3Sum

---

### 5. Two Pointers on Two Arrays
**Time Complexity:** O(n + m)
**Space Complexity:** O(1)

Examples:
- LC 349 Intersection of Two Arrays

---
